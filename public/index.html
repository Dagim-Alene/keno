How It Works

Each round’s 20 numbers are generated deterministically by the server using HMAC-SHA256 with the server seed as key and “clientSeed:nonce” as message. The result feeds an unbiased Fisher–Yates shuffle over the 80 numbers.

📖 Rules & Paytable

Classic 80-Ball Keno with Provably Fair HMAC-SHA256 draws

3 or More Numbers Hit Rule

• Picks 3 to 7: Hitting 3 or more numbers always awards a winning multiplier (from 1× push up to 3,000× your bet).
• Picks 8 to 10: Designed for high jackpots! Payouts trigger on higher tiers scaling all the way up to 10,000× your bet (e.g. 10 ETB wins 100,000 ETB!). On 10 picks, catching 0 numbers also pays a 2× consolation win.

How to Play:
1
Choose Numbers: Pick 1 to 10 numbers on the 1–80 grid, or tap Quick Pick.
2
Place Your Bet: Set your wager from 1 ETB up to 500 ETB using the bet chips.
3
Watch the Draw: 20 winning numbers are drawn live one by one. Matched tiles highlight in glowing teal.
4
Collect Winnings: Payout is calculated as Bet × Multiplier and immediately credited to your balance.
🛡️ 100% Provably Fair Guarantee

Every draw is predetermined using cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 from the server seed, client seed, and round nonce. You can independently verify all past rounds under the Fairness tab.
